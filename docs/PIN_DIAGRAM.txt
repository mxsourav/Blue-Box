================================================================================
                        ⚠️  PIN CONFLICTS
================================================================================

  CONFLICT #1:
    GPIO 10 → BTN_RIGHT (INPUT_PULLUP) vs CE1_PIN / nRF24 Radio1 (OUTPUT)
    GPIO 11 → BTN_DOWN (INPUT_PULLUP) vs CSN1_PIN / nRF24 Radio1 (OUTPUT)

    IMPACT : nRF24 Radio1 cannot operate while buttons use these pins.
             Radio2 (GPIO 12/13) is unaffected.
    RESOLUTION : Reassign CE1/CSN1 to free GPIOs, or disable Radio1.
